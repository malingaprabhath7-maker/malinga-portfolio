A free bilingual expense tracker: log daily spending, compare weekdays with the weekend and get a monthly summary — on phone or laptop, even offline.

Open live site Source code

Most budgeting apps are English-only and ask for a bank connection. I wanted something my family could use in Sinhala in ten seconds a day.

What it does

  • Accounts with Firebase, plus a guest mode that moves into your account later
  • Week and month views with weekday vs weekend totals and a safe daily amount
  • Shared family books with invite codes
  • Bill scanning on the phone itself with Tesseract.js
  • Budgets, repeating items, CSV and PDF export
  • Installable PWA that works offline, in light or dark mode

Built with

  • JavaScript (ES modules)
  • Firebase Auth
  • Cloud Firestore
  • PWA
  • Tesseract.js

My role

Solo project: design, front end, Firestore security rules and tests.
