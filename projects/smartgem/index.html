Final-year research project · 2026

SmartGem

An offline-first Sinhala and English mobile app that helps small-scale gem miners in Uva Province value their stones, share profits fairly with partners and keep their mining licences valid.

Small-scale miners often sell rough stones without knowing what they are worth, keep partner profit-sharing on paper, and miss licence renewal dates set by the National Gem and Jewellery Authority (NGJA). Many mine sites have weak mobile coverage, and many users are more comfortable reading Sinhala. Study area: Passara, Lunugala, Badalkumbura and Okkampitiya.

The approach

SmartGem keeps everything on the phone first, in an encrypted database, so it works deep inside a mine with no signal. When a connection appears, a Sync Manager quietly backs the data up to a central server. Every screen switches between Sinhala and English with one tap.

What it does

Partner share management

Built

Records mining costs and gem sales, then works out each partner’s share of the net profit. Unpaid overheads carry forward to the next period instead of being lost.

NGJA compliance

Built

Tracks mining and dealing licences with expiry badges, phone notifications 14 days before expiry, Sinhala and English email reminders and a PDF compliance report.

Licence guide

Built

Step-by-step guidance for mining and dealer licences, with a dealer fee calculator checked against all 20 official NGJA fee totals.

Gem library

Built

54 gem types with bilingual descriptions, identification and care tips, indicative LKR prices and a carat calculator that warns how much weight a rough stone loses when cut.

Secure accounts and cloud sync

Built

AES-256 encrypted local database with its key in the Android Keystore, password reset by email code, and background sync to MySQL over a JWT-protected API.

AI gem valuation

In progress

On-device gem identification with a MobileNetV2 model converted to TensorFlow Lite, trained on field photos labelled by a gemmologist. Price comes from the type, the weight and the price database.

Status

How it is built

  1. 1 · Phone

    Flutter app

    SQLCipher database, key in the Android Keystore, Sinhala and English UI

  2. 2 · Phone

    Sync Manager

    Uploads a snapshot when data changes, on app start and every 5 minutes

  3. 3 · Server

    Node.js + Express

    bcrypt passwords, JWT login, one transaction per sync

  4. 4 · Server

    MySQL 8

    9 tables with foreign keys, daily licence-reminder emails at 9:00
