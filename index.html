Final-year research project · 2026

SmartGem

An offline-first Sinhala and English mobile app that helps small-scale gem miners in Uva Province value their stones, share profits fairly with partners and keep their mining licences valid.

Small-scale miners often sell rough stones without knowing what they are worth, keep partner profit-sharing on paper, and miss licence renewal dates set by the National Gem and Jewellery Authority (NGJA). Many mine sites have weak mobile coverage, and many users are more comfortable reading Sinhala. Study area: Passara, Lunugala, Badalkumbura and Okkampitiya.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • SQLCipher
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • MySQL 8
